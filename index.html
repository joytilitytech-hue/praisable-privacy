Praisable Privacy Policy

From prayer to praise.
Effective date: September 27, 2026

Praisable is a personal prayer-management application developed by Joytility. Praisable is designed with a local-first approach so that your prayer information remains under your control.

Information Stored by Praisable

Praisable allows you to enter information such as prayer requests, names of people, groups, tags, prayer updates, follow-ups, answered prayers, praises, and related notes. This information is stored locally on your device. Joytility does not operate a server that receives or stores your prayer content.

Praisable does not require a Joytility account.

Voice Entry

Praisable provides optional voice entry using Android speech-recognition capabilities. Voice recognition is initiated by the user. Praisable uses the resulting transcription as text within the app and does not intentionally retain permanent audio recordings.

Speech-recognition processing may be provided by Android or speech services available on your device. Processing behavior can therefore depend on your device, Android version, and speech-recognition provider.

Google Play Billing

Praisable offers an optional one-time purchase of Praisable Full through Google Play. Purchase processing and ownership information are handled by Google Play. Joytility does not receive or store your payment-card information.

Praisable may query Google Play to determine whether the Google account using the app owns Praisable Full.

Sharing and Exports

Praisable allows you to deliberately share or export information. Depending on the features available to you, this can include formatted text, PDF files, Praisable transfer files, and manual backups.

Sharing and exporting occur only when initiated by you. When you choose another application, service, or storage location as a destination, information you send there becomes subject to that destination's privacy practices.

Backup and Restore

Praisable provides manual backup and restore functionality. Backups are created only when initiated by you and are saved to a location you choose through Android's system file interface.

Joytility does not receive or store these backups.

Permissions and Notifications

Praisable may request Android permissions needed for features you choose to use, such as microphone access for voice entry and notification permission for prayer follow-up reminders.

Praisable does not use these permissions for advertising or tracking. Reminder notifications are designed to avoid unnecessarily displaying prayer content.

Data Collection and Advertising

Praisable does not contain advertising and does not sell your personal information or prayer content.

Praisable does not use prayer content for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and Joytility does not operate a cloud service that receives your prayer database.

Data Security

Praisable provides privacy features including optional App Lock and the ability to hide sensitive app screens from Android's recent-apps view.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their device and for protecting backup or exported files after they leave Praisable.

Data Retention and Deletion

Prayer information remains on your device until you modify or delete it, restore different data, clear Praisable's application data, or uninstall the application, subject to Android's behavior.

Praisable does not maintain a Joytility user account or cloud prayer database requiring a separate account-deletion request.

Manual backups and exported files are controlled by you and must be deleted from their saved locations by you if you no longer wish to retain them.

Children

Praisable is a general-purpose personal prayer-management application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Joytility may update this Privacy Policy as Praisable changes. Material changes to Praisable's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy.
